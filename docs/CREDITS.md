# Third-party components and resources

- The exterior meshes and textures use MakeHuman Community geometry and Margaret Toigo’s CC0 skins. The male external pelvic surface uses registered BodyParts3D geometry. [Model credits](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
- **BodyParts3D 4.0**, © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0. Named bone surfaces supply the skull, shoulder girdles, rib cage, arms, hands and feet. The 14 costal cartilages form a separate group. The male model uses the source pelvis, long bones, pancreas and other abdominal organs. Teeth and auditory ossicles are outside this selection. [Source archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html) · [Licence](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html)
- **HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas**, CC BY 4.0. The male lung surfaces use 20 bronchopulmonary segments from the five lobes of the Visible Human male. Its spleen and 30 spinal cord segments also come from the male reference. The female scan supplies the two lungs, heart, kidneys, spleen, pancreas, pelvis, both femurs, kneecaps, tibiae and fibulae, and paired uterine arteries and veins. The female stomach, feet and remaining supplemental bones and vessels are fitted from BodyParts3D. [3D reference library](https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library) · [Female reference](https://lod.humanatlas.io/ref-organ/united-female/v1.10/)
- The BodyParts3D vessel groups collect 420 source arterial surfaces and 197 venous surfaces. Several surfaces can belong to one vessel. The separate jugular paths approximate the course alongside the carotids. The additional lower-limb veins use Z-Anatomy. Capillaries are outside the atlas. [Vascular model credits](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
- Face tracking: **Google MediaPipe**, a library under the **Apache-2.0** licence. See [the licence](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/LICENSE) and [the notes on the local files](../public/face/README.md).
- **React**, **Three.js**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Motion** and the other dependencies remain under their own licences, available in the npm packages.
- Typeface: **Barlow** and **Barlow Semi Condensed** (Jeremy Tribby), licence **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, served locally through the `@fontsource` packages.
- Webcam pulse: our own implementation of **POS** (Wang et al., 2017) and **CHROM** (de Haan and Jeanne, 2013), with the [papers and the protocol](VALIDATION.md). No code from **heartbeat-js** (GPL-3.0) or from **rPPG-Toolbox** (Responsible AI Source Code License 1.1) is included; the offline tests with published weights are described in [the experiment report](WEBCAM_RESEARCH.md).
- Scientific papers and datasets are cited in each signal's detail and in `docs/RESEARCH.md`. A citation is not a clinical validation of the software.
- EVA and IVA suit models by **NASA / Michael D. Carbajal**. [Original models and Blender adjustments](../public/human/SOURCES.md#spacesuits).
- Habitat photographs: **NASA**; CHAPEA photograph: **NASA/Bill Stafford**. The exact sources, the downloaded files and the context of each photograph are listed in [the image register](../public/environments/README.md). Educational use under the [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). The presence of these images does not mean that NASA approves or certifies this software.
- Spacewalk views of the AR view: Earth's limb with part of Japan's Kibo module, photographed from the ISS on 21 November 2020, **NASA** (`iss064e006316`); Orion with the Earth and the Moon on flight day 13 of Artemis I, **NASA** (`art001e000672`); Curiosity's self-portrait on Vera Rubin Ridge, **NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS** (`PIA22207`). Listed in [the image register](../public/environments/README.md).
- The 3D coach of the self-tests reuses the VRM sample of the avatar lab, under the same [terms](AVATAR_LAB.md).
- Additional references: Apollo 17 landscape, published credit **NASA, ESA and J. Garvin (NASA/GSFC)**; Curiosity panorama of Mars, **NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS**. The three interiors of the rover and the bases were generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool from these references and from the structure photographs above. These illustrations are marked as generated and are not attributed to NASA; their sources and prompts are documented in [GENERATION.md](../public/environments/GENERATION.md).
- Sample portrait: a fictional person generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool, labelled as such in the face studio and on the body view. Provenance and prompt: [SAMPLE.md](../public/face/SAMPLE.md).
- **Head fitting templates** (`src/body/portrait/referenceHeads.json`): derived locally from the credited BodyParts3D and HuBMAP reference meshes using MediaPipe Face Landmarker. Their source mesh attributions and CC BY 4.0 terms remain applicable. Reproduction and limitations: [FACE_FITTING.md](FACE_FITTING.md). These are generic reference people, not user biometric data.
- **Three-angle sample**: three renders of a modified BodyParts3D surface, © The Database Center for Life Science (CC BY 4.0), textured with the project's fictional portrait. [Credits and reproduction steps](../public/face/multiview/README.md).
- The avatar comparison uses **three-vrm** and **KalidoKit** (MIT) and a VRM model © pixiv Inc. The [sources, the model licence and the redistribution terms](AVATAR_LAB.md) are given with the protocol.

DECA and FaceMeshFaceGeometry are research references in [AVATAR_OPTIONS.md](AVATAR_OPTIONS.md); their code and models are not included.
